Privacy Notice

How information provided in connection with this website may be handled.

Last reviewed: October 2026. This notice describes the operation of this introductory website. Confirm the firm's full internal retention and compliance procedures before publication.

1. Who to contact

C.O. Were & Company Advocates, Witeithie House, 2nd Floor, Thika Town, Kenya. Email: cwerelaw@gmail.com.

2. Information you choose to provide

If you use the contact form, your browser assembles the name, email address, optional telephone number, topic and message into a draft in your own email application. This website does not itself transmit or store that form submission. If you then send the email, the information is received and processed via the parties' email services for the purposes of considering and responding to your enquiry.

3. Technical data

Hosting providers may process standard technical information, including IP addresses and request logs, to deliver, secure and operate this website. Google Fonts, if loaded, may receive technical request information. The map embed is provided by OpenStreetMap and may also collect standard connection data when displayed.

4. Purpose and confidentiality

Initial correspondence may be used to identify the nature of the enquiry, consider availability, communicate about a possible consultation and maintain necessary business records. Please do not include confidential case details before the firm has agreed to act.

5. Retention and rights

Retention periods depend on the nature of any subsequent relationship and applicable legal, professional and record-keeping requirements. For enquiries about access, correction or deletion of personal data, contact the email address above. Rights are subject to applicable Kenyan law, including the Data Protection Act, 2019.

6. Updates

This notice may be updated as the website, service providers or processing practices change.

Publication checklist: confirm this notice with the firm's data-protection contact, processing practices and retention schedule before launch.
